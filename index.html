Four parts, in the same order as on exam day.

The app follows the real exam from the first recording to the final score, with the official timing and question types.

  1. 1

    Hören

    Listen once or twice, exactly as in the exam. The audio starts on its own and doesn't rewind, so you practise under real conditions.

    Announcements, voicemails, conversations and radio interviews.

  2. 2

    Lesen

    Emails, ads, signs and newspaper articles, with the question types of the real paper: richtig/falsch, a/b/c, ja/nein and matching.

    Timed like the exam, with every text written for the level.

  3. 3

    Schreiben

    Write your email, form or forum post with a live word counter. Your text is scored on the official criteria for your level.

    Task completion, structure, vocabulary and grammar.

  4. 4

    Sprechen

    An AI partner plans with you, listens to your presentation and asks follow-up questions. You speak, it answers.

    Only your part of the conversation is scored.

  5. ✓

    Ergebnis

    Each module is scored the way the certificate is: points per part and whether you passed.

    See where you are strong and what to practise next.
